Privacy Policy

Wortham Business Tools operates Wortham Accounting CLI as a private reporting utility for authorized Little King Inc personnel. This policy explains how the utility handles information obtained through QuickBooks Online.

Information accessed

After an authorized user completes Intuit OAuth 2.0 authorization, the utility may access company information, chart-of-accounts data, transaction reports, uncategorized transactions, and Profit & Loss reports for the selected QuickBooks Online company.

How information is used

Information is used only to create internal accounting views and reports requested by an authorized user. It is not used for advertising, profiling, lending, insurance, payments, or sale to third parties.

Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens and application credentials are stored in macOS Keychain on the authorized computer.
  • Retrieved QuickBooks data is processed locally.
  • Report data is retained only when an authorized user deliberately exports or saves it.
  • This public website does not collect QuickBooks data and uses no analytics, advertising trackers, forms, or cookies.

Sharing

Wortham Business Tools does not sell or rent QuickBooks data. Data is not disclosed to third parties except when an authorized user directs it, when required by law, or when necessary to protect legal rights and system security.

Security

The utility uses Intuit OAuth 2.0, verifies OAuth state, uses a loopback callback, redacts secrets from normal output, stores credentials in macOS Keychain, and enforces GET-only QuickBooks accounting-data requests. No system can guarantee absolute security.

User control and deletion

An authorized user may revoke the connected app from QuickBooks Online and remove the local Keychain authorization. See the disconnection instructions. Any deliberately exported files must be deleted separately by the person who created them.
