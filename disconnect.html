Access removal

Disconnect and delete local authorization

An authorized administrator can remove the utility’s access to QuickBooks Online and delete the local authorization from the Mac.

1. Revoke the Intuit connection

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator.
  2. Open Apps or Connected apps.
  3. Find Wortham Accounting CLI and choose Disconnect or Revoke.
  4. Confirm the disconnection.

2. Remove local authorization

On the authorized Mac, open Keychain Access and delete the Wortham Accounting CLI item for the profile:little-king account under the com.via.qbo-cli service. This removes the locally stored OAuth authorization for that profile.

3. Delete exported reports

The CLI does not automatically retain retrieved reports. If an authorized user deliberately exported a report, delete that file separately from its saved location and any backups where deletion is required.

Result: After Intuit revocation and local removal, the CLI can no longer retrieve data for that QuickBooks company unless a user completes a new authorization.
