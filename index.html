Private accounting reports, without changing the books.

Wortham Accounting CLI is an internal command-line utility for authorized Little King Inc personnel. It retrieves selected QuickBooks Online accounting information for review and reporting while enforcing GET-only accounting-data access.

Read-only accounting workflow

What it reads

Company information, accounts, transaction reports, uncategorized transactions, and Profit & Loss reports.

What it cannot do

It cannot post, edit, delete, categorize, reconcile, or operate QuickBooks bank-feed workflows.

Local security

OAuth tokens and app credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Retrieved data is processed locally and retained only when an authorized user deliberately exports it.

Access

This utility is private and is not offered as a public accounting service. Access requires authorization from both Wortham Business Tools and Intuit.

Connection details

Operated by Wortham Business Tools for authorized Little King Inc personnel.
