Connect QuickBooks Online

Wortham Accounting CLI is a private internal tool. Connection requires approval from Wortham Business Tools and authorization from a user who has access to Little King Inc in QuickBooks Online.

Before connecting: Review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Connection steps

  1. On the authorized Mac, open Terminal.
  2. Run qbo connect --profile little-king.
  3. Sign in to Intuit in the browser window that opens.
  4. Select Little King Inc and approve the requested QuickBooks accounting connection.
  5. Return to Terminal and confirm that the company profile is connected.

Permission scope

Intuit’s accounting authorization may be broader than individual report calls. The local CLI nevertheless enforces GET-only accounting-data transport and refuses unsupported write and reconciliation commands.

Need to remove access?

Follow the disconnect and deletion instructions.
